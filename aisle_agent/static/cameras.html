Ring cameras

Your cameras stay in your Ring account. You approve Aisle Agent in the Ring app once; after that it can open a camera's live view and hear its motion alerts. Remove it from the Ring app to disconnect at any time.

How the video gets here

  1. Motion. The camera sees movement and Ring sends a signed alert, checked before anything happens.
  2. Live view. Aisle Agent opens the camera's live view on its server (WebRTC). Ring ends each view after 60 s on wired cameras and 30 s on battery ones; while shoppers are in frame, the next one opens.
  3. Only what changes the bill. Hands at a shelf zone are checked before and after: a take adds a line, a put-back flags one.
  4. Nothing is kept. Frames are analysed and dropped. Only the crops behind each bill line are kept, for the exit check.

Cameras with end-to-end encryption turned on can't be used: Ring doesn't share their video with any app.
